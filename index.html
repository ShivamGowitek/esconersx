Light Lag

Space and science, checked against the sources.

Light Lag is an independent YouTube channel. Each video takes one question about black holes, planets, telescopes, physics or the deep past of the universe, and follows the evidence to the answer. Every video lists its sources in its description.

Videos are produced with AI tools, including an AI narration voice. Any AI illustration is labeled on screen.

The Light Lag Pipeline app

Light Lag Pipeline is a private tool used only by the channel's operator. It connects to that operator's own YouTube account to upload the channel's videos and thumbnails, schedule them, and read the channel's analytics. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Light Lag is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or any space agency.
